🚥 ThreatBuddyForever (V2.0 - Ultimate Edition)
ThreatBuddyForever is a smart, ultra-lightweight multi-target threat monitoring addon designed specifically for the WoW Forever / Classic (Vanilla) game client [INDEX].
Instead of forcing you to stare at complex numerical tables or tiny standard interface text, this addon attaches a dynamic, glowing circular traffic light directly to the right side of floating enemy health bars (Nameplates) [INDEX]. This allows you to manage threat across massive pulls with a single split-second glance [INDEX].
🚀 Key Features (What It Does)
• Smart Role Inversion (Tank vs. DPS/Healer):
	• The addon automatically detects your current combat stance, talent specialization, or shapeshift form.
	• As a DPS/Healer: The signal turns Green when you are safe and changes to a pulsing Red if you rip threat (imminent danger).
	• As a Tank: The colors instantly invert! It glows Green when you have secure threat (your goal) and turns Red if an ally steals the mob from you.
• Cyan Neon Taunt Alert: Whenever a mob is struck by a Taunt ability (e.g., Growl, Mocking Blow, Provoke), the signal instantly switches to a Neon Cyan Blue color and displays TAUNT, letting you know the creature is hard-locked mechanically.
• Emergency Audio Warnings: Triggers a clean, high-priority system sound (Raid Warning) the exact millisecond a secondary mob rips threat onto you, giving you plenty of time to pop a cooldown or pull it back.
• Focused Target Highlight: The enemy you are currently targeting gets a customized, scaled-up widget (adjustable via slider) and displays your exact threat percentage (e.g., 85%) in a thick, highly visible font outline.
• Trivial Unit Filter: Automatically filters out and hides widgets on Totems and low-threat enemy Pets, keeping your combat screens exceptionally clean and clutter-free.
• Per-Character Profiles: Gives you the choice to save your settings account-wide or isolate your scales, offsets, and lag controls uniquely on a character-by-character basis.
• 7-Language Support: Fully translated at runtime into English, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, French, German, and Japanese.
🛠️ Clear Configuration Panel (The In-Game Menu)
Typing the command in chat opens a custom-built interface within the native WoW options window, featuring a glowing, code-animated neon Logo, a Vertical Scrollbar for seamless navigation, and the following controls:
• Interactive Checkboxes:
	• Enable Neon Glow: Toggles the pulsing background aura effect on critical states.
	• Hide While Solo: Disables the addon entirely when you aren't in a group or raid.
	• Allow Solo with Pet: Opens an exception for Hunters/Warlocks to see threat against their own pets.
	• Play Sound Alert / Highlight Target / Filter Totems / Use Per-Character Profile.
	• Includes a dedicated Play button next to the sound checkbox for instant acoustic testing.
• Organized Sliders (Stacked Vertically to Prevent Overlapping):
	• General Size (Scale): Controls the global size of the widgets on nameplates (0.5x to 2.0x).
	• Target Highlight Size: Customizes how large your active target's widget becomes (1.0x to 2.5x).
	• Lateral Distance: Adjusts how many pixels to the right the widget sits away from the health bar.
	• Update Frequency (Lag Control): Sets the script update speed. Slide it down to 0.02s for instantaneous real-time precision (Zero Lag), or up to 0.20s to drastically save CPU resources on older computers.
⌨️ Slash Commands (How To Use It)
You can manage the addon at any time by typing the following commands directly into your game's chat frame:
• /tbf — Instantly opens the customized options panel with the vertical scroll frame.
• /tbf test — Toggles a static test widget at 50% threat in the center of your screen, letting you adjust scales and distances safely outside of combat.
• /tbf scale [0.5-2.5] — Swiftly alters the baseline scale of the widgets via text input.
• /tbf reset — Emergency reset command that centers the testing frame back to default screen coordinates.
